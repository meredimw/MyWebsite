Independent Comedy • Workplace

American Job (1996)

Directed by Chris Smith • Low-wage work, dead ends, and Midwestern life.
Chris Smith’s dryly funny independent feature follows a young man drifting through a succession of unrewarding jobs while trying to determine whether adult life offers anything beyond bad shifts, indifferent managers, and the next small paycheck. Awkward, melancholy, and sharply observant—a natural companion to Smith’s later documentary American Movie.

Mystery Documentary

Resurrect Dead: The Mystery of the Toynbee Tiles (2011)

A street-level mystery about messages embedded in asphalt.
One of the great modern mystery documentaries. An investigation into the bizarre Toynbee Tiles embedded in streets across North and South America becomes a meditation on obsession, outsider art, and the people who refuse to let mysteries die.
