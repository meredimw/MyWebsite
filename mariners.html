October 10, 2025 • Winner-Take-All ALDS
Postseason Classic

Legendary 15-inning grinder at T-Mobile — winner goes to the ALCS, loser goes home. An instant entry in Mariners lore.

  • Walk-off to the ALCS: Jorge Polanco’s single in the 15th drove in the winning run and clinched the series for Seattle.
  • Refusing to go quietly: The M’s struck out 20 times but still scratched out 3 runs on 8 hits to win 3–2.
  • Clutch contributions: Leo Rivas delivered a game-tying RBI single, and Josh Naylor went 3-for-6 with a double and a stolen base.
  • Pitching marathon: After the 6th inning, Mariners pitching held Detroit scoreless over the final 9 innings of the longest winner-take-all game in postseason history.
