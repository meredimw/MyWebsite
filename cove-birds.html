Cove Birds

A catalog of birds observed around Murden Cove and nearby shoreline.

Pelagic Cormorant

Urile pelagicus
Seabird Cormorant Coastal resident

Quick facts

ID keysSlender neck, dark body, red facial skin in breeding season.
HabitatRocky shorelines, jetties, and offshore stacks.
BehaviorOften seen drying wings on rocks after diving.
Source: Audubon Field Guide

Bald Eagle

Haliaeetus leucocephalus
Raptor Apex predator Year-round resident

Quick facts

ID keysWhite head and tail, massive yellow bill.
Wingspan6–7.5 feet.
BehaviorSoars, perches conspicuously, steals fish from other birds.
Source: Audubon Field Guide

Bufflehead

Bucephala albeola
Duck Diver Winter visitor

Quick facts

ID keysSmall duck; male has large white head patch.
HabitatProtected bays, coves, and harbors.
BehaviorRapid dives; often surfaces far from entry point.
Source: Audubon Field Guide
