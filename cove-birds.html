Red-necked Grebe

Podiceps grisegena • Murden Cove

A large grebe often seen swimming or diving on open water. In breeding plumage it shows a chestnut-red neck; in winter it becomes grayer but keeps a strong face pattern.

Quick ID

  • Long neck and straight, stout bill
  • Dark cap with pale cheeks
  • Often rides low in the water

Behavior

  • Frequently dives rather than flying
  • Often solitary in winter
  • Can be surprisingly tolerant of nearby boats
