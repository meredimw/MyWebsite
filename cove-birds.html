Cove Birds

A catalog of birds observed around Murden Cove and nearby shoreline.

Red-necked Grebe

Podiceps grisegena
Waterbird Grebe Winter visitor

Quick facts

ID keys Pale cheeks, thick neck, yellowish bill; rides low in the water.
Size 18–20 inches long.
Habitat Coastal waters, protected bays, and nearshore salt water in winter.
Behavior Excellent diver; often resurfaces far from where it submerged.
Conservation IUCN: Least Concern.
Source: Audubon Field Guide Open Audubon Guide

Great Blue Heron

Ardea herodias
Wader Shoreline hunter Year-round resident

Quick facts

ID keys Large gray-blue heron with long legs, S-curved neck, and yellow bill.
Size ~4 feet tall; wingspan over 6 feet.
Habitat Shallow shorelines, tidal flats, docks, and estuaries.
Feeding style Ambush predator; slow steps followed by a rapid spear-like strike.
Flight ID Flies with neck tucked back — unlike cranes.
Source: Audubon Field Guide Open Audubon Guide
