Red-necked Grebe

Waterbird Grebe Winter visitor

Quick facts

ID keysPale cheeks, thick neck, yellowish bill.
Size18–20 inches long.
HabitatCoastal waters and protected bays in winter.
BehaviorStrong diver; resurfaces far from where it submerged.
StatusLeast Concern.
