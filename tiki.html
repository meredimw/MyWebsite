Radio Tiki

A tribute to Chris P.’s musical selections from the first episode of Radio Tiki.

Episode One • February 11, 2000

Broadcast from the Old Internet

The first Radio Tiki episode mixed jokes, dispatches from the early web, cultural detours, and a perfect little playlist: New Order, The Cure, Aimee Mann, Tori Amos, and Cocteau Twins.

Episode Highlights

  • Bob Dobbs introduces the H-Bomb
  • Tom’s trip to the “studio”
  • Stephen Hawking chimes in
  • We don’t need no stinkin’ FCC
  • Cheeks and the Buffy Report
  • Kevin Smith vs. Magnolia
  • Internet Under Siege
  • William “FREAKING” Gibson
  • Chris’ Spam Bin / Tales of the Old Internet
